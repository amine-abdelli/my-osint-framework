# Training, communities and practice

## Training and certification

| Provider | Offering |
| --- | --- |
| **SANS Institute** | **SEC487** — Open Source Intelligence Gathering and Analysis. Certification: **GOSI** (GIAC Open Source Intelligence) |
| **IntelTechniques** (Michael Bazzell) | Online courses, workshops, the *Open Source Intelligence Techniques* book series, and the *Privacy, Security & OSINT Show* podcast |
| **Trace Labs** | Missing-persons OSINT; CTF-style events; active community |
| **TCM Security** | Practical OSINT courses |
| **TryHackMe** | OSINT learning paths and dedicated rooms |
| **Cybrary** | Free security training including OSINT |
| **Basel Institute on Governance** | Free eLearning course on Operational Analysis — hands-on intro to the intelligence cycle |

## Books

1. **Michael Bazzell — *Open Source Intelligence Techniques*** — the reference handbook, updated regularly.
2. **i-intelligence — *OSINT Handbook*** — free; the 2018 edition is the source of this base's tool catalog. Current edition at `osinthandbook.com`. ⚠️ i-intelligence is a Swiss private OSINT training firm, not a government agency, despite the name.
3. **Christopher Hadnagy — *Social Engineering: The Science of Human Hacking*** — for understanding the human layer.
4. **Kevin Mitnick — *The Art of Invisibility*** — privacy and anonymity, useful for OPSEC.

Practice geolocation specifically. It is the skill that transfers most widely and the one
that degrades fastest without use.
